## 📝 SECTION 0: OFFICIAL HACKATHON DECLARATION (NHCE RULES 6.1, 6.2, 6.4.1, 7.1)

All team members of Project SUTRA (`SHIH26-TID-361`) formally affirm compliance with the following hackathon invariants:

1. **Originality & Zero-Plagiarism (Rule 6.2)**: All control algorithms (quintic splines, ORCA 3D velocity obstacles, C3BF barrier certificates), Deep JSCC neural models, 6-DOF WGS84 raycasters, and SwarmRAFT consensus protocols were formulated and implemented during the hackathon. Plagiarism or copying preexisting repos is strictly prohibited.
2. **Transparent AI/Tool Usage (Rule 6.4.1)**: AI assistants (Google DeepMind Antigravity CLI, Google Gemini, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, DeepSeek) were used strictly for boilerplate scaffolding, automated PyTest suite generation (255 passing tests), and documentation formatting. All architectural decisions, mathematical proofs, and system integrations are original and human-engineered.
3. **Open-Source Attribution (Rule 7.1)**: Full attribution to ROS 2 Humble/Jazzy, PX4 v1.14, Gazebo Sim 8, NVIDIA Sionna, PyTorch, TensorRT, YOLOv8, ByteTrack, and Mapbox GL JS under their respective open-source licenses.
4. **Mandatory Jury Feedback Incorporation (Rule 6.1)**: 100% of feedback from Evaluations 1 and 2 is incorporated and tracked in [`docs/hackathon/JURY_FEEDBACK_TRACKER.md`](docs/hackathon/JURY_FEEDBACK_TRACKER.md).
5. **Joint IPR Ownership (Rule 8.1 & 8.2)**: Solutions developed during the hackathon are jointly owned by NHCE and Team SUTRA in equal proportion.

## 🤝 SECTION 1: CONTRIBUTOR COVENANT CODE OF CONDUCT

### Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in Project SUTRA a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

### Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other team members and hackathon competitors
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive jury feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
* Focusing on what is best not just for us individually, but for the overall project team and institutional representation

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as physical or email addresses, without explicit permission
* Any violation of NHCE campus regulations, alcohol/drug bans, or property safety rules (Rule 5.5)
