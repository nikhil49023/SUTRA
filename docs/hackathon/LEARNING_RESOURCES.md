## Tips for Effective Learning

1. **Watch at 1.5x speed** — Save time, you can always slow down for complex parts
2. **Code along** — Don't just watch, type the code yourself
3. **Take notes** — Write down key concepts in your own words
4. **Teach each other** — Explain what you learned to your team
5. **Practice daily** — Consistency beats cramming
6. **Focus on understanding** — Not memorization, understanding why things work
7. **Ask questions** — Use forums, Discord, or ask your team
8. **Build something** — Apply what you learn immediately
