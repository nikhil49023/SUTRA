The Disaster Crisis & Search Bottlenecks

02 / 10

🚨 Real-World Disaster Pain Points (Kedarnath / Wayanad)

  • Severe GPS Denial: Mountain ravines, landslides, and forest canopy block satellite signals, causing single drones to drift and crash.
  • The "Digital Cliff Effect": Heavy RF multipath fading and jamming freezes traditional Wi-Fi and H.264 video streams at < 5 dB SNR.
  • Single-Drone Bottlenecks: Manual single UAVs lack wide search coverage, endurance, and failover when batteries deplete.
  • Swarm Collision Deadlocks: Naive potential fields oscillate or deadlock in narrow corridors, delaying critical life-saving rescue.

🎯 SUTRA Solution Architecture

  • Autonomous 5-UAV Swarm: Self-organizing multi-drone fleet executing coordinated wide-area sweeps without human piloting.
  • Sub-Millisecond Neural Disturbance Rejection: Proactively cancels 18 m/s turbulent wind gusts and rotor downwash.
  • Analog-Graceful Deep JSCC Video: Compresses thermal/RGB imagery by 96.9%, streaming continuous video even under -5 dB jamming.
  • Millimeter-Accurate Geolocation: Terrain-corrected DEM WGS84 raycasting geolocates survivors with < 0.32m error at 30m AGL.

SUTRA-FSD & Control Barrier Safety Shield

04 / 10

🚗 Tesla FSD-Style 3D Autopilot Planning

  • 3D Voxel Occupancy Grid ($32\times 32\times 16$): Temporal decay memory ($\lambda = 0.92$) preserves occluded obstacle positions.
  • Quintic Polynomial Spline Generator: Closed-form analytical solve ensures continuous curvature ($\mathcal{C}^2$ continuity, jerk-free).
  • 3D Echelon Altitudes: Staggers swarm vertically ($z \in [3.5\text{m}, 4.6\text{m}]$), eliminating central coplanar bottlenecking.

🛡️ Control Barrier Function (C3BF) Hard Shield

  • Forward Invariance Guarantee: Enforces $h(x) = \|\Delta p\|^2 - R_{\text{safe}}^2 \ge 0$ ($R_{\text{safe}} = 2.80\text{m}$, Gate G5).
  • Reciprocal Velocity Obstacle: $\vec{n}^T \vec{a}_i \ge -\left(0.5 v_{\text{closing}} + \frac{\gamma}{2d} h\right)$.
