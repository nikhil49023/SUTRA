Title:An Algebraic Characterisation for Finsler Metrics of Constant Flag Curvature
==================================================================================

Authors:[Ioan Bucataru](https://arxiv.org/search/math?searchtype=author&query=Bucataru,+I)
, [Dan Gregorian Fodor](https://arxiv.org/search/math?searchtype=author&query=Fodor,+D+G)

View a PDF of the paper titled An Algebraic Characterisation for Finsler Metrics of Constant Flag Curvature, by Ioan Bucataru and 1 other authors

[View PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.07476)

> Abstract:In this paper we prove that a Finsler metrics has constant flag curvature if and only if the curvature of the induced nonlinear connection satisfies an algebraic identity with respect to some arbitrary second rank tensors. Such algebraic identity appears as an obstruction to the formal integrability of some operators in Finsler geometry, \[4,7\]. This algebraic characterisation for Finsler metrics of constant flag curvature allows to provide yet another proof for the Finslerian version of Beltrami's Theorem, \[2,3\].
